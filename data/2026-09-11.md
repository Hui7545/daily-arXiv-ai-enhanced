### [1] [On the Regularization Landscape for the Linear Recommendation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11876)
*Dong Li,Zhenming Liu,Ruoming Jin,Hao Zhou,Zhi Liu,Jing Gao,Bin Ren*

Main category: cs.AI

Priority: 72

TL;DR: 本文研究多种基于深度学习的推荐算法性能接近的原因，发现其线性模型等价于引入核范数或Frobenius范数正则项，并提出两种低秩闭式解，兼顾两者优点。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 深度学习推荐算法虽技术各异（dropout、autoencoder等），但性能与代价函数高度相似，作者希望探究这种一致性是巧合还是可统一到一个框架下解释。

Method: 理论分析主流线性推荐模型的正则化本质，将其归纳为核范数正则（低秩、闭式解、结构刚性限制表达能力）与Frobenius范数正则（表达力强、但解为满秩或需ADMM等难调数值过程）两类；在此基础上对Frobenius范数正则进行推广，推导两种低秩闭式解。

Result: 揭示各领先线性模型实际仅添加核范数或Frobenius范数正则项；核范数类模型结构受限但解为闭式低秩，Frobenius类更富表达力、更高效但求解困难；提出的新解同时获得低秩、闭式与高效表达力。

Conclusion: 通过统一正则化视角可解释多种深度学习推荐算法的等价性能，并据此设计出兼具核范数与Frobenius范数优点的低秩闭式解，为推荐模型设计提供理论指导。

Abstract: Recently, a wide range of recommendation algorithms inspired by deep learning techniques have emerged as the performance leaders on several standard recommendation benchmarks. While these algorithms were built on different DL techniques (e.g., dropouts, autoencoder), they have similar performance and even similar cost functions. This paper studies whether the models' comparable performance are sheer coincidence, or they can be unified under a single framework. We find that all linear performance leaders effectively add only a nuclear-norm based regularizer, or a Frobenius-norm based regularizer. The former ones possess a (surprising) rigid structure that limits the models' predictive power but their solutions are low rank and have closed form. The latter ones are more expressive and more efficient for recommendation but their solutions are either full-rank or require executing hard-to-tune numeric procedures such as ADMM. Along this line of finding, we further propose two low-rank, closed-form solutions, derived from carefully generalizing Frobenius-norm based regularizers. The new solutions get the best of both nuclear-norm and Frobenius-norm world.

### [2] [Debate-to-Skill: Capability-Bound Process Supervision for Industrial Query-to-Agent Annotation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11176)
*Shiyu Zhang,Leisheng Cheng,Huifu Li*

Main category: cs.AI

Priority: 55

TL;DR: 该论文将工业查询到 Agent 的匹配问题建模为"能力边界的过程监督"（capability-bound process supervision），提出 Debate-to-Skill 标注方法，通过可复用决策原则、结构化辩论、验证器裁决抽取与分歧驱动的迭代改进进行标注，并在工业 Query2Agent 基准上与直接标签监督、推理式 SFT 及结构性消融进行对比。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 工业场景下查询到 Agent 的匹配常把"主题相关"误当作"可执行能力"，在长尾查询与边界敏感请求上尤为严重。作者认为问题根源在于监督信号只标注最终标签，未监督决定"能否执行"的关键决策过程，因此希望用过程监督来区分语义相关性与可执行能力。

Method: Debate-to-Skill 多阶段标注流程

Result: 仅描述实验设计，报告数值为定性

Conclusion: 过程监督有潜力提升边界查询匹配质量

Abstract: Industrial query-to-agent matching fails when topical relevance is mistaken for executable capability, especially on long-tail and boundary-sensitive requests. We formulate annotation as \emph{capability-bound process supervision} and instantiate it with Debate-to-Skill, which uses reusable decision principles, structured deliberation, verifier-based verdict extraction, and disagreement-driven refinement. On an industrial Query2Agent benchmark, we compare Debate-to-Skill with direct-label supervision, reasoning-SFT, and structural ablations. The results test whether gains come from supervising the capability-critical decision process itself, especially on grey-zone cases where semantic relatedness and executable capability diverge.

### [4] [UniRec: Cross-stage Multi-Task Fusion with Preference Alignment for Cascaded Recommender Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11052)
*Lingyuan Kong,Jiaqi Cui,Fanjiao Zeng,Congqi Wang,Yu Li,Yuan Cheng,Jingxin Liu,Xiaoshuang Chen,Kaiqiao Zhan*

Main category: cs.IR

Priority: 80

TL;DR: UniRec 提出一种跨阶段统一融合模型，将预排序与排序两个融合模块放入同一计算图联合训练，通过双向偏好对齐与属性分组相对正则化缓解跨阶段不一致，并在快手全量部署，线上 App 使用时长提升 0.616%。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 工业推荐系统采用多阶段级联架构，各阶段目标、特征空间和时延约束不同。预排序与排序分别独立优化会导致跨阶段不一致：上游可能过滤掉下游偏好的物品，而下游独立调优的融合又会抵消上游收益。已有工作多聚焦于排序阶段内部的多目标融合，或仅在跨阶段中引入下游分数因子，缺乏对两阶段融合模块的联合优化。

Method: 1）两个融合模块部分共享输入嵌入并在单一计算图中训练，使任一阶段的梯度都能经共享表示传播并影响另一阶段；2）双轴偏好对齐目标：纵向跨阶段一致性项将下游成对偏好迁移到上游融合分数，横向紧凑聚合项将异构先验信号上的数十个成对目标重组为双向偏好证据；3）针对端到端融合优化可能利用物品属性分布不均衡、过度集中于高收益区域的问题，引入属性分组相对正则化，在属性组内计算优势并在同一组内归一化策略，使整体提升某一高收益组无法获得优化增益。

Result: 离线实验中 UniRec 持续优于单阶段融合与跨阶段协同基线；线上 A/B 测试显示 App 使用时长提升 0.616%；系统已在快手平台全量部署。

Conclusion: 跨阶段融合模块的联合优化可有效缓解级联推荐中的跨阶段不一致，双轴偏好对齐配合属性分组相对正则化兼顾了多目标均衡，方案在工业级大规模场景中验证了收益并完成落地。

Abstract: Industrial recommender systems use cascaded stages with different objectives, feature spaces, and latency constraints. Optimizing pre-ranking and ranking separately can create cross-stage inconsistency: upstream models may filter out items preferred by downstream rankers, and independently tuned downstream fusion can offset upstream improvements. Existing multi-task fusion methods focus on multi-objective fusion within the ranking stage, and cross-stage methods typically only add a downstream score factor to upstream ranking. Joint optimization of fusion modules across both stages remains largely unexplored.
  We propose UniRec, a Unified Cross-stage Recommendation Fusion model. First, the two fusion agents partially share input embeddings and are trained in a single computation graph, so gradients from either stage propagate through the shared representation and influence the other. Second, we introduce a dual-axis preference alignment objective: a vertical cross-stage consistency term transfers downstream pairwise preferences to the upstream fusion score, and a horizontal compact aggregation term reorganizes dozens of pairwise objectives over heterogeneous prior signals into bidirectional preference evidence. Third, we find that unconstrained end-to-end fusion optimization can exploit imbalances in item attribute distributions, over-concentrating on high-reward regions at the cost of other objectives. We therefore add an attribute group-relative regularization that computes advantages within attribute groups and normalizes the policy over the same groups, so uniformly promoting an entire high-reward group yields no optimization gain. Offline, UniRec consistently outperforms single-stage fusion and cross-stage coordination baselines. Online A/B tests show a 0.616\% gain in app usage duration. UniRec is fully deployed on the Kuaishou platform.

### [5] [FedHUR: Learning Hierarchical Utility-Guided Client Relations for Personalized Federated Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11632)
*Mingzhe Han,Jiahao Liu,Dongsheng Li,Jiankui Zhou,Hansu Gu,Peng Zhang,Ning Gu,Tun Lu*

Main category: cs.IR

Priority: 72

TL;DR: FedHUR：以item-item滤波器为聚合对象，利用分层效用信号指导客户端关系的构建与个性化聚合。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有联邦推荐个性化聚合方法基于预定义的参数相似性或互补性假设，仅构建单一全局关系，难以刻画用户关系的分层与多粒度特性，且预定义关系无法直接反映相关客户端聚合后能否真正提升预测性能。

Method: FedHUR以item-item filters作为关系构建与聚合的对象：先对各客户端本地信息进行聚合与聚类，得到全局分层信息；各客户端基于本地信息与全局分层信息计算分层效用信号，指示哪些协作信息有利于提升自身预测；服务器据此效用信号检索对该客户端有用的客户端，进行进一步的个性化聚合。

Result: 在五个真实世界数据集上的大量实验表明，FedHUR持续优于现有的联邦推荐基线方法。

Conclusion: 实验验证了分层效用引导的客户端关系学习的有效性，可作为联邦推荐中个性化聚合的通用思路；代码已开源。

Abstract: Federated recommendation enables collaborative model training while keeping user interaction data on local clients. A central problem in federated recommendation is how to aggregate useful information across clients for personalized recommendation. Existing personalized aggregation methods usually construct client relations from predefined parameter-based assumptions, such as parameter similarity or complementarity, and use these relations to determine aggregation weights. However, such methods construct a single global relation, which is insufficient to capture the hierarchical and multi-granularity nature of user relations in recommendation. Moreover, these predefined relations cannot directly reflect whether the related clients can improve prediction performance after aggregation. To address these limitations, we propose FedHUR, a federated recommendation framework for learning hierarchical utility-guided client relations. FedHUR takes item-item filters as the object for relation construction and aggregation. Specifically, it first aggregates and clusters each client's local information to obtain global hierarchical information. Each client computes hierarchical utility signals based on its local information and the global hierarchical information, indicating which collaborative information is useful for improving its prediction. The server uses these utility signals to retrieve clients that are useful to that client for further personalized aggregation. Extensive experiments on five real-world datasets show that FedHUR consistently outperforms existing federated recommendation baselines, demonstrating the effectiveness of hierarchical utility-guided client relation learning. Code is available at https://github.com/Mingzhe-Han/FedHUR.

</details>
